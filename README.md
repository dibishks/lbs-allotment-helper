# LBS Allotment Helper

A simple static tool that helps Kerala LBS Centre applicants estimate which colleges they
might be allotted, based on the **previous year's allotment list** index marks..

🔗 **Live:** https://dibishks.github.io/lbs-allotment-helper/

## How it works
- Enter your **Index Mark** (out of 300) and optionally pick your **quota**.
- The tool lists colleges where last year's *last-allotted* candidate scored at or below your mark,
  split into **Government** and **Self-financing** tables.
- Click any college to view its full allotment list.

## ⚠ Disclaimer
Predictions are based **purely on the 2025 allotment data** and are **indicative only** — they are
**not a guarantee** of allotment. Actual cutoffs vary each year. Always rely on the official
LBS Centre allotment for final decisions.

## Tech
Static site — `index.html` + `data.js`. No backend. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

*Currently covers B.Sc. Nursing; other courses to be added.*
